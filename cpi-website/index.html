FAQ

Common questions

How long does a home inspection take?

Most single-family homes take 2–3 hours depending on size, age, and condition. Larger or older homes may take longer.

Do I need to be present for the inspection?

It's not required, but we encourage it. Joining for the last 30 minutes lets us walk you through our findings in person.

When will I receive my report?

Reports are delivered digitally within 24 hours, and often the same evening. You can easily share it with your agent or attorney.

Should I get a radon test in Cleveland?

Yes, we strongly recommend it. Many Northeast Ohio counties have elevated radon levels, and the only way to know is to test.

Why is a sewer scope important for older homes?

Many Cleveland-area homes were built before 1960 with clay or cast-iron sewer lines that can crack, collapse, or fill with roots. Repairs can cost thousands, and a standard inspection can't see inside the line.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, checks, and digital payments. Payment is due at or before the time of inspection.
